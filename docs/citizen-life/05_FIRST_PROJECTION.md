# First Projection

Carrying Assets in a store is not the same as becoming visible. Projection is the moment the Citizen’s sealed contents are materialized into a form the living home can present.

## What occurred

After the genesis Assets were installed and the Manifest sealed, the Projection Engine ran against that Manifest. Projection slots appeared under the living home. Birth marked *Projection Ready*. The Terminal spoke of preparing projection; the Journal recorded that what was carried had become visible.

## Why it occurred

The Runtime must not invent institutional knowledge. It may only project what signed Assets authorize. First Projection proves the Asset → Manifest → materialization path works at Birth — before Sync ever adds new parts.

## What changed

The Citizen gained a projection plane: readable outputs derived from Assets, refreshable on boot and after updates. Boot again re-projects from the current Manifest.

## What remained

Identity and Evidence were already present. Projection did not rename the Citizen. It did not rewrite Evidence. It added a visible consequence of the Manifest.

## What evidence exists

- Evidence: `BIRTH_PROJECTION_READY` (and later boot projection reports).  
- Telemetry: `birth.projection` with duration and slot names when recorded.  
- Timeline node *Projection*.  
- Files under the living `projection/` plane.  
- Birth Package inclusion of projection artifacts.

First Projection is not “the UI product.” The Observatory UI is a separate witness window. Projection is the Asset materialization plane that proves the Citizen can show what it carries without an LLM.
