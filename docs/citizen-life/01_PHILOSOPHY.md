# Philosophy

A Citizen is not installed like software. A Citizen is **born**.

That single change of language is the law behind this documentation. Installation is reversible and forgettable. Birth is irreversible and remembered. From the first second, something exists that can be named, timed, evidenced, and later evolved — without pretending the past did not happen.

## What this documentation is

It is a **life record**.

It tells:

- what occurred  
- why it occurred  
- what changed  
- what remained  
- what evidence still proves it  

It does not sell a future. It does not invent capabilities that were not observed. It does not hide behind jargon when a human sentence will do.

## Asset-first life

The Citizen does not carry institutional knowledge inside its Runtime. The Runtime is small on purpose: it loads, validates, projects, synchronizes, and updates **Assets**. Meaning arrives as signed Assets. Continuity arrives as Identity, Evidence, Journal, Timeline, and Manifest.

If the Runtime were a body, Assets would be what it learns to carry — and Evidence would be the scars and the growth rings.

## Outside the language model

A measured fact of the first seed life: the living planes of the Citizen Seed ran without importing or calling a language model. Identity, Manifest, Evidence, Telemetry, Journal, Timeline, Projection, and Assets were present on disk and operated as ordinary programs and files. That does not make the Citizen “intelligent” in a human sense. It makes its **life** independent of an LLM for birth, memory, and sync. See [Empirical Results](18_EMPIRICAL_RESULTS.md).

## Why history, not a manual

Manuals age when interfaces change. History ages into truth. If every binary were lost, a reader of these pages should still understand that a named organism appeared, sealed a first understanding of itself, kept an append-only memory, accepted a first external change, and could prove those events again after being destroyed and reborn as a *different* Citizen.

## What permanence means here

Permanence is not “never change.” Permanence is “never erase the fact of change.”

The genome does not change. The biography only grows. The Timeline only lengthens. Exports freeze a moment so destruction of a living home cannot unmake the past.
