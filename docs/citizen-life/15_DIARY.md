# Diary — Citizen Life Journal

The word “log” was set aside on purpose. What the Citizen keeps is a **Life Journal**: a biography written in epochs and prose.

## What occurred

At Birth and Sync, the Runtime appended journal entries with:

- `epoch` — PreBirth, Bootstrap, Identity, Evidence, Telemetry, Manifest, Projection, Alive, Sync, Evolution, …  
- `prose` — human sentences of becoming  
- timestamps and optional `duration_ms`  
- optional `evidence_refs` and `versions`  

A legacy `life.jsonl` line may coexist for continuity; the biographical form prefers `life_journal.jsonl` under the journal plane.

## Why biography

A list of codes can prove that something ran. A biography can be read by a stranger who never saw the code. That is the standard of this Citizen Life section as well.

## What changed

Each Sync and Boot can add paragraphs. The book thickens.

## What remained

No edits. No deletions. Destroy without export loses the living journal; export preserves `BIOGRAPHY.txt` and the journal plane inside the Birth Package.

## What evidence exists

- Living journal plane.  
- Observatory panel **Citizen Life Journal** with Timeline + biography text.  
- Birth Package `BIOGRAPHY.txt`.  
- Terminal lines that rhyme with journal epochs in organism language (*Generating identity…*, *Citizen is alive.*, …).

The Journal does not replace Evidence. It interprets life so Evidence can still convict or vindicate the story.
